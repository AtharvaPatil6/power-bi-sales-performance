# Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze sales performance across products, categories, regions, and months.

The dashboard focuses on key business metrics such as sales, profit, orders, and profit margin, with interactive filters for Region and Category.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- DAX
- CSV
- Data Cleaning

## Key KPIs
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin

## Dashboard Analysis
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top 3 Products by Sales
- Profit by Category
- Profit Margin by Category
- Interactive Region and Category slicers

## Key Insights
- Electronics generated the highest overall sales and profit.
- Electronics had the highest overall sales contribution among the categories.
- Profitability differs across categories when comparing total profit with profit margin.
- Sales performance varies across regions and months.
- The top-performing products contribute significantly to overall sales.
